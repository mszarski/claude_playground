About

This is a real Nintendo 64 ROM (built with libdragon and tiny3d), running in EmulatorJS with a mupen64plus core patched to support modern libdragon homebrew. Graphics are emulated pixel-accurately in software, so it needs a fast computer; expect it to run below full speed on phones.

Collect the three Seals of Shamash on the steppe of Uruk, open the cedar door, and face Humbaba, guardian of the Cedar Forest.
