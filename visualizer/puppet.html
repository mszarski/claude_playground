Puppeteer Reachy

Act out a feeling with your face and head; Reachy copies you. Record it, name it, and it becomes training data for how Reachy moves. Your camera image stays on your phone: only the robot's motion is saved.

Tips: hold the phone at eye level, relax, tap "Reset neutral", then perform. Turn, nod, tilt and raise or lower your head; raise or furrow your brows for the antennas. Up to 15 s per clip. Face tracking: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0).
